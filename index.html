Inside the portal

Everything an LP needs, in one console.

VentureOS runs the full fund lifecycle on-chain — sourcing, diligence, committing, and reporting — so your position is always verifiable, never a PDF in your inbox.

Deal flow

Vetted TokenOS-native rounds with sponsor memos, terms, and the diligence trail before allocation opens.

Data room

Cap tables, token schedules, legal, and founder updates — permissioned to your tier, watermarked to your wallet.

Allocations

Commit and track pro-rata directly from the console. Escrow, vesting, and pull-forward all settle on-chain.

LP reporting

Live NAV, MOIC, and IRR per position. Distributions stream to your wallet the moment an exit clears.

Governance

Investment-committee votes run on-chain. Higher tiers sponsor deals and shape which rounds the fund backs.

Syndicate

Co-invest alongside the fund, share intros, and access founder office hours with the rest of the LP base.
